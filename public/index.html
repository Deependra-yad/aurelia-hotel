The Sanctuary

Restored Limestone Elegance, Designed for Unhurried Living

Carved out of a 1924 Beaux-Arts residence and reimagined by Milanese architects, Aurelia balances monumental travertine arches with tactile warmth—hand-oiled walnut, Belgian linen, and tranquil olive courtyards.

Triple-glazed acoustic stonework isolates every room from the avenue outside, while private French terraces open directly onto centuries-old Mediterranean cypress and our serene central reflecting pool.
